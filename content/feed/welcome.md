# SAS Apps Blog gets a Feed

Short-form updates from the SAS Apps team now live under the **Feed** tab on
the [blog](/blog) - quick announcements, releases and links, in the style of
the [Data Controller feed](https://datacontroller.io/feed).

Long-form articles stay right where they are, under **Articles**. Existing
post URLs are unchanged.
